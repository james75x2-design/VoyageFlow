# VoyageFlow Travel Insurance Guidance

## What Travel Insurance Typically Covers

VoyageFlow does not sell, broker, or provide travel insurance, but it encourages travelers to consider a policy before booking. Common travel insurance coverage includes:

- **Trip cancellation and interruption** — reimbursement for prepaid, non-refundable costs when a trip is canceled or cut short for a covered reason
- **Emergency medical and evacuation** — treatment costs and transport if you fall ill or are injured abroad
- **Baggage loss or delay** — compensation for lost, stolen, or delayed luggage
- **Travel delay** — expenses such as meals and lodging during covered delays

Coverage varies by provider and plan. Travelers must read the policy terms directly.

## What VoyageFlow Does Not Do Regarding Insurance

VoyageFlow **does not**:

- Sell, underwrite, or act as a broker for any travel insurance product
- Recommend a specific insurer, plan, or coverage amount
- Provide advice on whether a given policy is adequate for a traveler's situation
- Process claims, refunds, or reimbursements of any kind

VoyageFlow may surface a general reminder to consider insurance, but any purchase happens directly with a licensed insurance provider. Travelers are responsible for choosing and verifying their own coverage.

## Choosing and Verifying a Policy

Before purchasing travel insurance, VoyageFlow encourages travelers to verify the following directly with the insurer:

- Covered reasons for cancellation and interruption, and any exclusions
- Medical coverage limits, deductibles, and whether pre-existing conditions are included
- Emergency evacuation limits and the claims process while abroad
- Coverage window relative to trip dates, and any deadlines to purchase after booking
- Documentation required to file a claim, such as receipts and reports

Policy terms, limits, and pricing are set by the insurer and can change without notice.
